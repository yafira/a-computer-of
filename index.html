a computer of ___

A generator of soft computers: imaginary machines made of fabric, powered gently, built without urgency. Each stanza assembles one machine in seven steps: material, binding, power, interface, place, inhabitant, promise.

my words is the original generator: seven lists of phrases written from my own design practice. our words picks each slot from the nearest neighbors of a target feeling in a semantic corpus hand-tagged by the Reading and Writing Electronic Text class (Allison Parrish, NYU ITP), then refuses any word the class tagged cold, sharp, or academic. Refused words are struck through as the machine is built.
